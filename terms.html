01The service

Plenty is a personal expense tracker. You log expenses by typing, speaking, or photographing receipts; Plenty uses artificial intelligence (AI) to turn those inputs into structured expense records, tracks recurring subscriptions you tell it about, and shows you summaries and short observations about your own spending.

You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live) to use Plenty.
